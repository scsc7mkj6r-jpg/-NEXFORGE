# NEXFORGE — Game Modes

## 1. Team Deathmatch
- Two teams: Alpha and Bravo.
- Teams compete to reach the score limit.
- The team with the highest score wins.

## 2. Domination
- Teams capture and hold strategic objective zones.
- Holding zones earns team points.

## 3. Dynamic Battlefield
- Objectives can change during a match.
- Weather and visibility can change.
- Routes and map areas can open or close.
- Teams must adapt to changing objectives.

## Future Modes
- Search and Destroy
- Battle Royale
- Custom Matches
